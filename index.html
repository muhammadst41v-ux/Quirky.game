How to play
  1. Move: tap one of your pieces, then tap a green dot. Pieces move one step along the lines.
  2. Capture: jump over an enemy piece into the empty spot right behind it (red dot). You can keep jumping in a row.
  3. Diagonals only work where a diagonal line is drawn.
  4. Opening shield: the piece you move on your first turn cannot be captured on the next turn.
  5. King & Commander mode: losing your King knocks you out. A Commander has two lives, and can jump over two enemy pieces standing in a row (shown with x2).
  6. Build your army (7x7, 9x9): some pieces are fixed. Add the same number of extra pieces inside your own highlighted area, then tap Done.
  7. Triangle (5x5): tap your triangle, then an empty spot in your start area to bring in a reserve piece.
  8. The glowing path and ghost circles show the last move, and which pieces were captured.
